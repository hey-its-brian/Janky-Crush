# Backdrop prompts

The zone backdrops were made with the Z-Image Turbo model
(`mcp-tools/Z-Image-Turbo` on Hugging Face), size 864 x 2016, seed 21,
8 steps. Use these prompts to make them again or to add a zone.

Each prompt has the same structure:

- The top quarter shows above the board: sky, hills, trees.
- The middle is calm water. The board covers it.
- The bottom quarter shows below the board: water, rocks, small objects.

All the prompts start with:

> Background art for a casual match-3 mobile puzzle game, tall portrait.

All the prompts end with:

> Stylized 3D cartoon render like a top mobile puzzle game, soft clay-like
> rounded shapes, very vivid saturated candy colors, glossy, smooth
> gradients, warm sunlight from the upper left, gentle depth haze. No
> characters, no animals, no people, no text, no UI.

## zone1.webp: Yuzu Valley

> A cozy hot spring lagoon in a lush green valley on a sunny morning. Top
> quarter: vivid blue sky with fluffy white clouds, rounded bright green
> hills, a distant snow-capped mountain with a puff of steam, citrus trees
> full of orange yuzu fruit and palm tree crowns at the left and right
> edges, pink flowers. Middle: calm open turquoise water with soft wisps
> of steam. Bottom quarter: clear steaming turquoise hot spring water,
> smooth rounded gray rocks at the left and right corners, a few floating
> orange yuzu fruits, small pink flowers on the bank.

## zone2.webp: Coconut Cove

> A tropical beach cove with a warm lagoon on a bright sunny day. Top

> small green island, tall coconut palm trees leaning in from the left and
> right edges, a little thatched beach hut far away. Middle: calm open
> turquoise lagoon water. Bottom quarter: clear shallow turquoise water
> over golden sand, smooth rounded rocks at the left and right corners,
> seashells, a starfish, a floating coconut.
